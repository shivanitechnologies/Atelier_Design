Architectural Methodology

Methodology Behind Timeless Sanctum

A transparent 5-stage turnkey workflow that honors European craftsmanship, client budgets, and transforms spaces with architectural serenity.

01 Stage 01 • 2 to 3 Weeks

Discovery & Consultation

We understand your lifestyle rhythms, family dynamics, aesthetic inclinations, budget parameters, and site conditions. We conduct in-depth laser site measurements and structural audits.

Key Deliverables:

• Comprehensive Client Brief & Lifestyle Analysis

• 3D Laser Measured Survey & As-Built Plans

• Preliminary Budget & Feasibility Road-map

02 Stage 02 • 3 to 4 Weeks

Concept Development

We translate spatial intentions into visual reality: moodboards, tactile material trays (stone, timber, metals, linens), spatial circulation options, and preliminary volumetric 3D concepts.

Key Deliverables:

• Curated Material Trays Delivered to Client

• 2D Spatial Layout Schemes (Option A & B)

• 3D Conceptual Mood & Atmosphere Renders

03 Stage 03 • 4 to 6 Weeks

Design Planning & Drawings

Every detail is engineered with millimeter precision. We produce complete architectural construction sets, bespoke millwork specifications, MEP electrical schedules, and sanitaryware schedules.

Key Deliverables:

• Full CAD Architectural Construction Drawing Pack

• Millwork & Joinery Fabrication Drawings

• Lighting, Switching & Acoustic Schedules

04 Stage 04 • Variable Duration

Execution & Coordination

Our architectural project directors oversee on-site implementation. We coordinate general contractors, specialist stone masons, joiners, and electricians with rigorous weekly quality audits.

Key Deliverables:

• Weekly Photographic Site Progress Reports

• Contractor RFI & Milestone Sign-Offs

• Material Sourcing & Guild Workshop Audits

05 Stage 05 • 1 to 2 Weeks

Final Styling & Handover

White-glove delivery, furniture placement, art hanging, architectural lighting calibration, scent curation, and turnkey champagne reveal. Your sanctuary is fully prepared to be inhabited.

Key Deliverables:

• Complete Turnkey Furniture & Art Dressing

• Bound Architectural Sanctuary Manual

• 10-Year Studio Quality Warranty Handover
