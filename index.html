01 / Studio Manifesto

Curators of Spatial Harmony & Architectural Silence.

At ATELIER, we discard transient interior fads in pursuit of timeless architectural composition. Every residence, penthouse, and corporate boardroom is approached as a three-dimensional sculpture—where light, raw travertine, smoked oak, and natural linen coalesce.

Founded in 2014 by architect Elena Vance, our multidisciplinary practice brings together licensed architects, bespoke furniture joiners, and lighting theorists across London and Zurich. We guide our clients through a frictionless journey from conceptual moodboarding to the ultimate turnkey handover.

04 / Our Ethos

Form Follows Emotion

We believe architecture should calm the spirit. Three non-negotiable tenets govern every pencil stroke and material sample.

Spatial Purity

Eliminating visual noise so natural illumination, architectural lines, and breathing room become the true centerpiece of the dwelling.

Tactile Materiality

Honest materials—raw travertine stone, brushed bronze, Belgian linen, and French smoked oak that develop a noble patina across decades.

Timeless Restraint

Rejecting fleeting viral fashion in favor of proportions, ergonomics, and subtle details that feel just as compelling thirty years from now.
