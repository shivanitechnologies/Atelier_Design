Our Heritage & Practice

Conceiving Environments of Lasting Poise

Founded on the premise that interior architecture shapes psychological equilibrium. We craft private sanctuaries with spatial purity, material honesty, and bespoke artisan execution.

The Origins

Born from an Obsession with Space, Light & Silence.

In 2014, architect Elena Vance established ATELIER following a decade leading landmark restorations across Florence, Zurich, and London. Frustrated by the disposable consumer trends in interior decoration, she envisioned a studio where the gravitas of classical architecture merges with modern minimalism.

Over the past twelve years, ATELIER has grown into an international boutique studio of twelve specialized practitioners: licensed architects, bespoke millwork designers, structural coordinators, and lighting artists. We intentionally restrict our intake to guarantee absolute immersion in every project.

12 Years Of Architectural Curation & Bespoke Living

Our Mission

To rescue residential and commercial interiors from transient decorative clutter. We construct holistic sanctuaries that celebrate acoustic calm, tactile earthen materiality, and intuitive spatial choreographies tailored to each client's lifestyle.

Our Vision

To champion a renaissance of architectural craftsmanship in contemporary interior design. We forge lifelong alliances with European stone quarries, timber mills, and metal guilds to ensure our creations age with graceful nobility.

Founder & Principal

Elena Vance

RIBA, SIA • Creative Director

"Architecture is not merely the shell that contains our lives; it is the silent orchestrator of our thoughts, breath, and intimacy."

Trained at ETH Zurich and the Architectural Association in London, Elena spent her formative years collaborating with master stonecutters in Carrara and timber craftsmen in the Black Forest. Her distinct vision strips away superficial ornament to let spatial geometry, sunlight paths, and natural patinas speak for themselves.
