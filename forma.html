01 / Spatial Philosophy

Design is not just what you see.
It is how a space makes you feel.

Every room possesses an acoustic cadence, a thermal presence, and an emotional gravity. We approach interior architecture through the lens of timeless restraint—stripping away visual noise until only authentic materials, purposeful geometry, and natural shadow remain.

“Simplicity carried to an extreme becomes elegance. A home should whisper its luxury rather than shout it.”

Elena Rostova • Principal Architect & Founder

04 / Material Dialogue

Tactile Honesty. Pure Provenance.

We build with materials that age gracefully, gaining character and deep patina through decades of human touch.

Tivoli • Italy

Honed Travertine

Warm, porous limestone quarried from historic Roman beds. Cross-cut and honed to a velvety matte tactile finish.

Ardennes • Belgium

Smoked European Oak

Slow-fumed using traditional ammonia smoking, drawing deep chocolate undertones naturally from inside the wood fiber.

Birmingham • UK

Burnished Brass

Hand-waxed, unlacquered solid brass handles and hardware that gently darken and evolve an individual living patina.

Tuscany • Italy

Vegetable-Tanned Leather

Hand-stitched full-grain cognac leather upholstery paired with matte charcoal acoustic casework.
