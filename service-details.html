Residential projects with ATELIER begin far beyond superficial decor. We assess solar paths, circulation dynamics, sound resonance, and structural boundaries. Our team drafts comprehensive construction drawings, custom joinery specs, and MEP coordinates before a single hammer is raised.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning ATELIER.

How does ATELIER handle structural modifications & council approvals?
Our in-house architects manage all statutory paperwork, structural engineering calculations, and heritage/conservation area planning applications with municipal authorities across the UK, Switzerland, and the EU.
Can we procure furniture and materials through your trade accounts?
Yes. Clients gain transparent access to our extensive European trade network, saving significantly on bespoke Italian upholstery, Scandinavian lighting, and custom joinery fabricators.
What is the typical investment range for a full turnkey residence?
Our full-service commissions typically range from £150 to £450 per square foot depending on structural interventions, custom millwork complexity, and material selections. We provide fixed-price design phases prior to execution.
How many concurrent projects does the studio accept?
We limit our studio strictly to twelve active estates each year. This guarantees that our principal architects personally direct your spatial layouts and conduct on-site milestone audits.
